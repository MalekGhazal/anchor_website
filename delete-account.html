Your data

Delete your Anchor account and data

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The short version: you can delete everything yourself, in about ten seconds, from inside the app. It's permanent and can't be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data.

Delete it from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Anchor and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Choose Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm. If the app asks you to sign in again first, do that and repeat the step. This is a security check before anything is removed.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details, including any linked Google or email account
  • Your streak, XP, level, and avatar choice
  • Every check-in and note you wrote
  • Your achievements and challenge progress
  • Your onboarding answers
  • Your leaderboard entry

What may linger briefly

Anchor stores data with Google Firebase. Based on Google's published retention periods, crash reports can be kept for about 90 days, and backups of deleted data can take up to about 180 days to be fully purged. During that time the data isn't used by Anchor and can't be restored by us.

If you can't open the app

If you linked a Google account or email to Anchor, send a message from that same address to malek@webelle.ca with the subject Delete my Anchor account, and we'll delete the account within 30 days.

If you've only ever used Anchor anonymously, there's no email or name attached to your account, so we have no way to confirm which account is yours. The in-app button is the only way to delete an anonymous account. If you've lost access to the phone, the account will simply sit unused.

Questions

You can read exactly what Anchor collects in the Privacy Policy, or email malek@webelle.ca.
