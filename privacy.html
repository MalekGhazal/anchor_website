Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The short version: Anchor collects only what the app needs to work. You can use it anonymously, with no name or email. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never share your data with advertisers. You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it from inside the app.

This policy explains what the Anchor mobile app ("Anchor", "the app", "we") collects, why, where it's stored, and the choices you have. It also covers this website.

What the app collects

  • An account identifier. When you first open Anchor, an anonymous account is created through Firebase Authentication. It's a random ID, not your name or email.
  • Optional sign-in details. If you choose to link a Google account or an email and password (for example, to keep your progress on a new phone), we store the email address and display name provided by that sign-in. This is optional.
  • Your recovery data. The information you create while using the app: your streak start time and longest streak, XP and level, achievements you've unlocked, and challenge progress.
  • Your check-ins. Each check-in you save, as much or as little as you choose to fill in: a quadrant for how you're feeling (energy and pleasantness) and, optionally, a more specific word for it; an urge level from 0–10; trigger tags; context tags for what you were doing, who you were with, and where — including any you type in yourself, which can include people's names if you choose to include them; and a short note. Only the first step is required; everything else can be skipped.
  • Your onboarding answers. If you answer the optional questions when you start (such as your reasons for quitting, when urges tend to happen, what helps, and a sentence about your "why"), we store those answers so the app can personalize tips and show your "why" in Reset mode. Every question can be skipped.
  • A leaderboard entry. A randomly generated username, your streak start time, and the date of your last check-in are stored in a leaderboard record. The app shows other signed-in users only your username and streak, and hides members who haven't checked in for several days. Your real name and email are never part of it.
  • Avatar choice. The avatar style and a seed value (by default, your random username) used to generate your picture.
  • Crash and stability reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects technical information such as device model, operating system version, and the error that occurred, so we can fix bugs. Your anonymous account ID may be attached to a crash report so we can investigate a problem you ran into.
  • Technical data processed by Firebase. When the app connects to Google's servers, Firebase handles technical information such as your IP address and a device installation identifier. Google keeps authentication IP logs for a few weeks.

What we don't collect

  • We don't access your photos, contacts, messages, or browsing history.
  • We don't track what you do in other apps or on other websites.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers or ad trackers, and there are no ads in Anchor.
  • We don't require your real name at any point.

How we use it

We use your data only to run the app's features: showing your streak and progress, syncing across devices if you've linked an account, personalizing tips from your onboarding answers, displaying the anonymous leaderboard, updating the home screen widget, and fixing crashes. We don't use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to anyone.

Sensitive information

Because Anchor is about a personal and sensitive topic, we treat everything you enter as private. Your check-ins — including any custom tags or notes you type in, which can mention people in your life by name if you choose to — and your onboarding answers are only readable by your own account, and our database security rules prevent other users from accessing them. The only record other users' devices can read is your leaderboard entry: a random username, your streak start time, and your last check-in date. The app displays only the username and streak.

Where your data is stored

App data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Our database is hosted in Google Cloud's Middle East region. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Firebase's standard security. The home screen widget keeps a copy of your streak count on your device so it can display it. Reminder settings and notifications are stored and scheduled on your device only.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers only to run the app:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database, and crash reporting.
  • Google Sign-In, only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • DiceBear to generate avatar images. Your device requests the image from DiceBear's servers, so DiceBear can see your IP address along with the avatar style and seed (by default your random username). No name, email, or account ID is sent.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your data with anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, so your progress is there when you come back. When you delete your account, your data is permanently removed from our database. Google's Firebase may keep crash reports for about 90 days and backups for up to about 180 days before they are fully purged, according to Google's published retention periods.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Profile and choose "Delete my account and data". This permanently deletes your account, check-ins and any saved check-in tags, achievements, challenges, onboarding answers, and leaderboard entry. It can't be undone. Full instructions are on our delete your data page.
  • Skip anything optional: onboarding questions, notes, and account linking are all optional.
  • Ask us: you can contact us to ask what data we hold about you, or to request that it be corrected or deleted. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or similar privacy laws.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and its fonts are hosted on the same server, so visiting it doesn't send your information to third parties. Our hosting provider may process basic technical data, such as IP addresses in server logs, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Age

Anchor is intended for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
